## Communication and meetings

* **Main communication channel:** Messenger group chat
* **Response expectation:** Within a few hours, or by the end of the day.
* **Regular meeting time:** Mostly during class, with additional Messenger check-ins.
* **Recording decisions:** Important decisions and action items will be recorded in GitHub Issues.
* **Absence or delays:** Members should inform the Messenger group as early as possible.

## How we work

* Create or update a GitHub Issue before starting significant work.
* Every task should have an owner and a clear Definition of Done.
* Link relevant evidence and implementation work to GitHub Issues.
* Important changes should be reviewed by another team member before merging.
* Give feedback respectfully and focus on the work, not the person.
* Help team members when they are blocked.
* Follow the Project Work Policy for privacy, attribution, and responsible AI use.

## If work or communication breaks down

1. Raise the concern privately and respectfully.
2. Identify the problem, evidence, and next action in the relevant Issue.
3. Agree on a recovery plan and review it at the next meeting.
4. If the issue remains unresolved, contact the instructor through the course-designated channel.

By signing, each member agrees to communicate problems early, contribute fairly, follow the team's working process, and update this agreement when circumstances change.
