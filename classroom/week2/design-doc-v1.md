# Design Doc v1

**Team:** Team 1  
**Project name:** Student Life Hub  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-14  

## 1. Project purpose

What problem are you solving?

Students often use different apps or websites for studying, finding food, looking for part-time jobs, and finding housing. Our project combines these useful services into one simple PWA so students can manage important parts of student life in one place.

## 2. Target users

Who is this for?

- Primary user: College and university students
- Secondary user, if any: Students who are new to a city or campus

## 3. Smallest useful version

What is the smallest version that would still be useful?

A PWA where students can view their study information, find nearby restaurants, search for part-time jobs, and browse housing listings.

### Rough user flow — 3–5 steps

`Start → Open Student Life Hub → Choose a service → Search or browse → View useful information`

Evidence / sketch link: [sketch](https://github.com/CapstoneDesign-Fall2026-UlsanCollege/Team1/blob/7a3a3412765bb2ab0f66a49634ec541a2a6012b8/classroom/week2/benko%20ss.png)

## 4. In scope

What are you building this semester?

- Student dashboard with schedules, assignments, and deadlines
- Restaurant search with basic information, categories, and prices
- Part-time job search with filters such as location and working hours
- Housing search with filters such as location, rent, and facilities
- Responsive PWA that works on desktop and mobile

## 5. Out of scope

What are you **not** building this semester?

- Real payment or booking systems
- Real-time job or housing applications
- Advanced roommate matching
- Full restaurant ordering or delivery
- Complex social networking features

## 6. Midterm demo sentence

Our midterm demo will show:

> A working PWA with a student dashboard and basic Study, Food, Jobs, and Housing sections with search and filtering.

## 7. Final demo sentence

Our final demo will prove:

> Students can use one PWA to manage basic student activities and easily find food, part-time jobs, and housing information.

## 9. Risks and unknowns

| Risk / unknown | Why it matters | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Project becomes too large | Combining four ideas can create too many features. | Focus on a small MVP and add features only if we have time. |
| Finding reliable data | Food, jobs, and housing information may not always be accurate. | Start with sample/mock data and consider APIs later. |
| Different features need different designs | The four sections may feel disconnected. | Use one common layout and design system. |
| Limited development time | We have a limited semester schedule. | Divide tasks between team members and track them with GitHub Issues. |
| Learning React and PWA | Some team members may be unfamiliar with the technology. | Learn the basics together and build features step by step. |
