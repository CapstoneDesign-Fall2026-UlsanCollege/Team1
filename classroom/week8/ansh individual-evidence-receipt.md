## Evidence of contribution

### 1. Housing Advertisements
I contributed to the idea of adding a housing advertisement feature to support students looking for accommodation near the university.

- Student-friendly room listings
- Monthly rent and deposit information
- Location and transport information
- Featured housing advertisements
- Verified listing options
- Example revenue: ₩20,000–₩80,000 per listing
- Revenue model: Listing fees or advertising packages
- Housing listings should be checked appropriately, and any paid housing services must comply with applicable Korean laws and regulations

This idea could help students find accommodation more easily while offering the platform a practical and scalable revenue stream.

### 2. Student Discount Partnerships
I also contributed to the idea of partnering with local businesses to offer student discounts through StudentHub.

- Café discounts
- Restaurant deals
- Gym membership offers
- Stationery discounts
- Student shopping promotions
- Example revenue: ₩50,000 per month for a sponsored discount campaign
- Revenue model: Business partnership fees and sponsored offers

This model offers value to students while giving local businesses a way to attract student customers through a trusted platform.

### 3. Institutional Reports and Analytics
I participated in developing the idea of providing institutional reports and analytics to universities.

- Timetable usage statistics
- Job-search activity reports
- Popular student service categories
- Campus service engagement
- General platform usage trends
- Revenue model: Paid institutional reporting and analytics packages
- Privacy requirement: Student data must be handled responsibly. Use aggregated information, protect student privacy, and never sell identifiable student data

This revenue model supports universities by providing useful data insights while protecting student privacy through aggregated and responsible data handling.
