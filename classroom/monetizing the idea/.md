StudentHub is a student-life platform for Ulsan College. It connects student profiles, courses, timetables, part-time job search, and favourite jobs in one place. Future features include Campus Eat and Room Finder.

The goal is to generate revenue while keeping essential student services affordable and accessible.

1. Paid Job Listings

Employers pay to publish part-time job opportunities on StudentHub.

Basic job listings can remain free.

Employers can pay to promote their listings.

Promoted jobs can appear at the top of search results.

Students can search for jobs that fit their class schedules.

Example revenue: ₩10,000–₩30,000 per promoted listing.

Revenue model: Employers pay a listing or promotion fee.

2. Local Business Advertising

Local businesses near Ulsan College can advertise their products and services to students.

Restaurants and cafés.

Convenience stores and shops.

Gyms and fitness centres.

Student-friendly services.

Example revenue: ₩30,000–₩100,000 per month per advertiser.

Revenue model: Monthly advertising fees.

3. Campus Eat Promotions

Campus Eat can help students discover affordable food and campus canteen offers.

Promote daily menus and special meals.

Highlight student discounts.

Feature special offers from participating restaurants.

Display sponsored menu items.

Example revenue: ₩20,000–₩50,000 per month for featured promotions.

Revenue model: Sponsored menu placements and promotional campaigns.

4. University Licensing

StudentHub can offer its platform to universities for an annual or semester-based fee.

Student profile management.

Course and timetable management.

Job-search services.

Administrator tools.

Academic information management.

Example revenue: ₩3 million–₩10 million per year for a small pilot institution, subject to negotiation.

Revenue model: Annual institutional licensing fees.

5. Premium Student Subscription

Keep the essential features free and offer optional premium services.

Advanced timetable planning.

Personalized job alerts.

Additional schedule reminders.

Enhanced job-search filters.

Example revenue: ₩1,000–₩3,000 per month per subscriber.

Revenue model: Monthly subscriptions.

Essential academic information and basic job searching should remain free whenever possible.

6. Room Finder for Institutions

Room Finder can help university administrators identify available classrooms using recorded class schedules.

Search by day and time.

Check for timetable conflicts.

Identify available classrooms.

Generate room availability reports.

Example revenue: An additional ₩500,000–₩2 million per year, depending on the features provided.

Revenue model: Paid administrative tools or an institutional software package.

7. Resume and Job Application Services

StudentHub can provide optional tools to help students prepare for part-time jobs.

Resume and CV templates.

Korean CV formatting resources.

Cover-letter templates.

Interview preparation materials.

Optional resume review services.

Example revenue: ₩3,000–₩10,000 per template or review package.

Revenue model: One-time purchases or service fees.
