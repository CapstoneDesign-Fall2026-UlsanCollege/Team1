# Five Project Ideas

**Team:** Team 1  
**Week:** 1  

Write five possible project ideas. Keep each one small.

## Idea 1

- **Project name:** Student Life Hub
- **Target user:** College and university students
- **Problem:** Students often use different apps or notes to manage their class schedules, assignments, exams, and deadlines.
- **Smallest useful version:** A PWA where students can add classes, assignments, and deadlines and view them in one simple dashboard.
- **Midterm demo could show:** Student dashboard, class schedule, assignment tracker, deadline reminders, and responsive PWA interface.
- **Big risk / unknown:** Managing multiple features while keeping the application simple and easy to use.

## Idea 2

- **Project name:** CampusEats
- **Target user:** College students looking for affordable food near campus
- **Problem:** Students may have difficulty finding nearby restaurants, affordable meals, menus, and opening hours in one place.
- **Smallest useful version:** A PWA that displays nearby restaurants with basic information, menus, prices, categories, and opening hours.
- **Midterm demo could show:** Restaurant search, category filters, restaurant details, menu display, favorites, and location-based results.
- **Big risk / unknown:** Keeping restaurant information, prices, menus, and opening hours accurate and up to date.

## Idea 3

- **Project name:** CineFlow
- **Target user:** Students and movie/TV fans
- **Problem:** Users need an easy way to discover movies and TV shows based on trends, genres, ratings, and personal interests.
- **Smallest useful version:** A PWA that allows users to search for movies, view details, and save movies to a personal watchlist.
- **Midterm demo could show:** Trending movies, search, movie details, ratings, genres, favorites/watchlist, and responsive design.
- **Big risk / unknown:** Finding a reliable movie API and handling API limits while keeping search and movie information fast.

## Idea 4

- **Project name:** StudentWork
- **Target user:** College students looking for part-time jobs
- **Problem:** Students often struggle to find part-time jobs that match their available time, location, and preferred working conditions.
- **Smallest useful version:** A PWA where students can search and filter part-time jobs by location, working hours, salary, and job category.
- **Midterm demo could show:** Job search, filters, job details, favorites, student profile, and application/bookmark functionality.
- **Big risk / unknown:** Keeping job information accurate and designing a system that can support both students and employers.

## Idea 5

- **Project name:** UniStay
- **Target user:** College students looking for housing or roommates
- **Problem:** Students may have difficulty finding affordable housing and compatible roommates near their school.
- **Smallest useful version:** A PWA that allows students to browse housing listings and filter them by location, rent, deposit, and available facilities.
- **Midterm demo could show:** Housing search, filters, listing details, photos, favorites, location/map, and basic roommate matching.
- **Big risk / unknown:** Implementing location-based search and roommate matching while keeping the project manageable within the semester.
